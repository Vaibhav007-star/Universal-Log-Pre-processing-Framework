How the AegisLog Backend Works

A simple, crystal-clear explanation designed for academic evaluators, professors, and non-technical reviewers. Understand the entire data pipeline in 2 minutes.

💡
The 10-Second Summary (Explain Like I'm 5)

In defense organizations like NTRO, security firewalls, routers, servers, and radar sensors produce heterogeneous logs — meaning every brand writes in a totally different format, timestamp format, and vocabulary. Analysts waste hours manually deciphering them before they can spot a cyber attack.

✈️ The Real-World Airport Customs Analogy
Think of the AegisLog backend like an International Airport Immigration Terminal:

• Incoming Logs = International Travelers: Some speak English (JSON), some speak French (Cisco), some speak Japanese (CEF), and some speak completely novel, unwritten dialects (military radar sensors).
• Fast-Track Biometric E-Gates (Hot Path): Known travelers pass through in less than 0.2 milliseconds via pre-compiled rules.
• The AI Linguist (Cold Path): If a traveler speaks a brand-new dialect never seen before, the AI learner analyzes their grammar (Drain3 algorithm), writes a new rule, and saves it so future travelers from that dialect pass through fast-track.
• The Universal Entry Card (OCSF v1.1): Every traveler's details (Who are they? Where did they come from? What did they do?) are transcribed onto ONE universal standard entry form.
• The Security Watchlist (MITRE ATT&CK): The system checks if the traveler's behavior matches known cyber threats and assigns a risk score.
• Officer Inspection Desk (Human-In-The-Loop): If any document is blurry or uncertain (score < 60%), a human customs officer inspects and approves it with 1 click in the dashboard.
🔄
The 6-Step Journey of a Log
1
Fast-Track Format Identification (Hot Path)
The pipeline instantly checks if the log matches known formats (Cisco ASA, ArcSight CEF, IBM QRadar LEEF, RFC 5424 Syslog, Linux Auditd, or JSON). Known logs parse in under 200 microseconds with zero AI overhead.
Folder: parsers/deterministic/
2
Autonomous AI Template Learning (Cold Path)
If the log is completely new or unseen, the Drain3 algorithm mines its structural skeleton. It dynamically generates a new regular expression, tests it in a secure sandbox, and saves it into our SQLite rule store so the next log of this type parses on the Fast Track.
Folder: inference/
3
Fallback Safety Net (Zero Silent Drops)
If both Fast Track and AI learning fail (e.g., corrupted garbage bytes), the fallback parser rescues every valid IP address, timestamp, and port number. The verbatim 100% raw text is preserved forever for legal defense integrity.
Folder: parsers/fallback/
4
Universal OCSF v1.1 Normalization & Threat Enrichment
All extracted fields are translated into international standards: timestamps become ISO-8601 UTC, IP addresses are classified as Private vs. Public, actions become ALLOW/DENY/DROP, and attacks are correlated against MITRE ATT&CK techniques.
Folder: core/normalizer.py
5
Multi-Level Calibrated Confidence Scoring
The system grades its own accuracy across 4 dimensions: Format Confidence (0-100%), Field Validity (0-100%), Schema Completeness (0-100%), and Overall Composite Score (0-100%).
Folder: core/confidence.py
6
Storage & Human-In-The-Loop Routing
High-confidence logs (≥ 60%) are written straight to our embedded DuckDB columnar database for sub-second SQL threat hunting. Low-confidence logs (< 60%) enter the analyst triage queue for 1-click human verification.
Folder: storage/ & hitl/

Top 4 Questions Teachers & Evaluators Ask
❓ Q1: Why is your backend so fast (38,000+ EPS) while others struggle?
Answer: Because we use a Deterministic-First architecture. We do NOT pass every log through slow AI or LLMs. Over 98% of known logs go through compiled regex plugins taking under 0.2 milliseconds. We only activate the AI learner when a genuinely novel, unseen format arrives.
❓ Q2: Does your system require internet or external LLMs to work?
Answer: No, it is 100% air-gap safe. Defense networks cannot send sensitive logs to external cloud APIs. Our heuristic learner runs locally in memory using the Drain3 clustering algorithm. External LLMs (Ollama or Gemini) are completely optional plugins.
❓ Q3: What happens when an incoming log is corrupted or broken?
Answer: We have a Zero Silent Drop guarantee. Our fallback extractor rescues valid IPs, ports, and timestamps, tags the log with diagnostic error codes, and preserves the verbatim 100% raw text for forensic chain-of-custody.
❓ Q4: How does the system improve over time?
Answer: When an analyst approves a corrected log in the Human-In-The-Loop triage queue (Dashboard Tab 4), that single click automatically compiles a permanent rule into our SQLite store and hot-reloads it into the active memory registry. The next time that log format arrives, it runs at full speed without human intervention!
